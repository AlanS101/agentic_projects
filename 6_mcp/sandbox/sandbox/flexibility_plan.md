# Flexibility Plan for Butterfly Kicks and B-Twists

## Goal
Improve overall flexibility, mobility, and body control to support learning **butterfly kicks** and **B-twists** safely and effectively. This plan focuses on hip mobility, hamstring flexibility, thoracic spine rotation, ankle mobility, glute strength, and dynamic control.

---

## Important Note
Before starting, if you have pain, prior injuries, dizziness, back issues, or joint instability, consult a qualified coach, physical therapist, or physician. Train skills like butterfly kicks and B-twists on appropriate surfaces and ideally with supervision.

---

## What These Skills Require
Butterfly kicks and B-twists are not only “tricking” skills — they depend on a combination of:

- **Hip flexibility** for wide, extended leg lines
- **Hamstring mobility** for clean kicking mechanics
- **Adductor flexibility** for straddle openness
- **Thoracic spine rotation** for turning and spotting
- **Ankle and calf mobility** for takeoff and landing mechanics
- **Glute and core strength** for power and control
- **Dynamic flexibility** so range of motion is usable at speed

Because of that, your plan should include both **mobility/flexibility work** and **active strength**.

---

## Weekly Structure
Use this schedule for **8–12 weeks**.

### 5-Day Structure
- **Day 1:** Lower body mobility + active flexibility
- **Day 2:** Thoracic rotation + technique prep
- **Day 3:** Lower body mobility + strength
- **Day 4:** Light recovery mobility
- **Day 5:** Dynamic flexibility + tricking prep drills
- **Day 6:** Optional light mobility or full rest
- **Day 7:** Rest

### Session Length
- **Full sessions:** 35–50 minutes
- **Recovery sessions:** 15–25 minutes

---

## Warm-Up Before Every Session (8–12 min)
Do not stretch cold.

1. **Light cardio** – 2–3 min  
   Jog, jump rope, or brisk movement.

2. **Joint prep** – 2 min  
   - Ankle circles x 10 each direction
   - Hip circles x 10 each direction
   - Arm circles x 10 each direction
   - Cat-cow x 8

3. **Dynamic mobility** – 4–6 min  
   - Leg swings front/back x 12 each leg
   - Leg swings side/side x 12 each leg
   - Walking lunges x 10 steps
   - Deep squat pry x 30 sec
   - World's greatest stretch x 4 each side

---

## Main Flexibility Priorities

### 1. Hamstrings
Helpful for high leg lift, cleaner lines, and safer kicking mechanics.

**Exercises:**
- Standing pike fold – 3 x 30 sec
- Half split rock-backs – 2 x 10 each side
- Seated single-leg fold – 2 x 30 sec each side
- Active straight leg lifts – 2 x 10 each leg

**Coaching note:** Keep spine long when possible instead of rounding aggressively.

### 2. Adductors / Middle Split Range
Critical for butterfly kick openness and aesthetic shape.

**Exercises:**
- Frog stretch – 3 x 30–45 sec
- Wide straddle fold – 3 x 30 sec
- Cossack squats – 2 x 8 each side
- Standing straddle pulses – 2 x 15

**Coaching note:** Think about rotating from the hips, not forcing the knees.

### 3. Hip Flexors and Quads
Needed for open hips, better takeoff posture, and reduced low-back compensation.

**Exercises:**
- Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch – 3 x 30 sec each side
- Couch stretch – 2 x 30 sec each side
- Low lunge with side bend – 2 x 20 sec each side
- Glute squeeze in lunge position – 2 x 10 each side

**Coaching note:** Tuck pelvis slightly to feel the stretch in front of the hip.

### 4. Glutes and External Rotation
Important for controlling leg paths and stabilizing landings.

**Exercises:**
- Figure-4 stretch – 2 x 30 sec each side
- 90/90 hip switches – 2 x 10 total
- Pigeon stretch or variation – 2 x 30 sec each side
- Clamshells or band abductions – 2 x 15 each side

### 5. Thoracic Spine Rotation
Essential for butterfly kick entry, torso alignment, and B-twist turning mechanics.

**Exercises:**
- Open books – 2 x 8 each side
- Thread the needle – 2 x 8 each side
- Lunge with rotation – 2 x 6 each side
- Wall thoracic rotations – 2 x 8 each side

### 6. Ankles and Calves
Supports stable takeoff, smoother pivots, and safer landings.

**Exercises:**
- Knee-to-wall ankle mobilization – 2 x 10 each side
- Calf stretch straight knee – 2 x 30 sec each side
- Soleus stretch bent knee – 2 x 30 sec each side
- Single-leg calf raises – 2 x 12 each side

---

## Strength for Active Flexibility
Passive flexibility alone will not transfer well to kicking and twisting. Add these 2–3 times per week:

- Hollow hold – 3 x 20–30 sec
- Side plank – 2 x 20 sec each side
- Glute bridge or single-leg bridge – 3 x 12
- Romanian deadlift (bodyweight or light load) – 3 x 8
- Reverse lunges – 2 x 8 each side
- Standing leg lifts to front and side – 2 x 10 each direction
- Superman hold or back extension – 2 x 20 sec

---

## Skill-Specific Mobility Drills
These help bridge flexibility into butterfly kick and B-twist technique.

### For Butterfly Kick
- **Butterfly kick entry step drill** – 3 x 5 each side
- **Chest-down sweeping leg drill** – 3 x 5 each side
- **Tilt drill over a line or low surface** – 3 x 5
- **Wide sweeping crescent kicks** – 2 x 8 each side

Focus on:
- Leaning the chest appropriately
- Opening hips during the sweep
- Keeping the kicking legs long
- Looking through the motion instead of throwing the head blindly

### For B-Twist Prep
- **Butterfly kick to controlled landing** – 3 x 4
- **Butterfly kick pop-up drill** – 3 x 4
- **Swing-through takeoff drill** – 3 x 5
- **Tornado kick / cheat setup drills** – 2 x 5 each side
- **Spotting turn drill** – 2 x 6

Focus on:
- A strong, committed set from the butterfly kick motion
- Core tension in the air
- Rotation generated from timing and body alignment, not arm flailing

## Progression Over 8–12 Weeks

### Weeks 1–2
- Learn positions
- Keep intensity moderate
- Do not force range
- Focus on consistency

### Weeks 3–5
- Increase hold times to 40–60 sec where appropriate
- Add more active leg lift height
- Increase quality of sweeping drills

### Weeks 6–8
- Add deeper straddle and hamstring work gradually
- Increase power in butterfly kick drills
- Practice stronger pop from butterfly kick into twist prep

### Weeks 9–12
- Maintain flexibility gains
- Emphasize dynamic application
- Increase skill practice volume carefully if joints feel good

---

## How Hard Should Stretching Feel?
Aim for about **6–7/10 intensity**.

- Mild to moderate discomfort is okay
- Sharp pain is not okay
- Numbness, pinching, or joint pain means stop and adjust

You should finish sessions feeling looser, not wrecked.

---

## Cooldown (5–8 min)
After training:
- Easy forward fold – 30 sec
- Hip flexor stretch – 30 sec each side
- Figure-4 stretch – 30 sec each side
- Open books – 5 each side
- Deep breathing – 1–2 min

---

## Extra Tips for Faster Progress
- Train flexibility **consistently**, not aggressively
- Film your butterfly kick mechanics weekly
- Prioritize **clean butterfly kicks before serious B-twist attempts**
- Work both sides for balance, even if one side is dominant
- Sleep and hydration matter more than people think
- If flexibility improves but skills do not, the missing piece is likely **technique, timing, or power**, not more stretching

---

## Simple Benchmarks
Track these every 2 weeks:
- Pike fold depth
- Straddle width
- Comfort in frog stretch
- Height of straight-leg side lift
- Quality of butterfly kick line on video
- Ability to land butterfly kick with control

---

## Final Advice
To do strong butterfly kicks and eventually B-twists, your best combination is:

1. **Dynamic warm-ups**
2. **Lower-body flexibility work**
3. **Active flexibility and strength**
4. **Thoracic rotation mobility**
5. **Consistent technical drills**

If you stay consistent for 8–12 weeks, you should notice better range, cleaner leg lines, stronger entries, and improved readiness for twisting skills.
